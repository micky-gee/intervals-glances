Privacy policy

Intervals Glances is a Garmin Connect IQ widget that displays your own intervals.icu data on your watch.

  • Your wellness data travels directly between your watch (via the Garmin Connect phone app) and the intervals.icu API. It is stored only on your watch, and only the most recent 90 days.
  • When you link your account, a small token-exchange service converts the one-time intervals.icu authorization code into an access token. It holds the app's client secret, processes each request in memory, and stores nothing.
  • The app has no analytics, no tracking, and no server-side storage of any kind. Nobody but you (and intervals.icu, where your data already lives) sees your data.
  • Revoke access at any time in intervals.icu → Settings → Apps.
